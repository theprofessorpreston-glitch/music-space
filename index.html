    About local cleaning

    The first run downloads a speech-recognition model. Deep clean also downloads a larger vocal-separation model. Processing speed depends on your phone. Cleaned copies are cached on this device when possible.

    AI can miss words. Review important songs before relying on the result.

    Privacy & terms

    Your audio files

    Files you select stay on this device. Selected files and the queue reset after a reload. Clean Mode, shuffle, and cleaning preferences can be saved in this browser.

    Local Clean Mode

    When you choose Prepare clean version, the browser runs speech recognition on the selected audio and edits a clean copy locally. The song is not sent to a paid cleaning service. The speech model may be downloaded from its public model host. Cleaned copies can be cached in this browser. AI can miss words, so review results before relying on them.
